What We Want to Achieve by 2030

Ensure that all children

complete free, equitable and quality primary and secondary education leading to relevant and effective learning outcomes, and have access to quality early childhood development, care and pre-primary education

(Targets 4.1 & 4.2)

Ensure equal access

for all women and men to affordable and quality technical, vocational and tertiary education, including university

(Target 4.3)

Substantially increase

the number of youth and adults who have relevant skills, including technical and vocational skills, for employment, decent jobs and entrepreneurship

(Target 4.4)

Eliminate gender disparities

in education and ensure equal access to all levels of education and vocational training for the vulnerable, including people with disabilities, indigenous people and children in vulnerable situations

(Target 4.5)

Ensure as many people as possible

including all youth, achieve literacy and numeracy

(Target 4.6)

Ensure that all learners

acquire the knowledge and skills needed to promote sustainable development, including, human rights, gender equality, promotion of a culture of peace and non-violence, global citizenship and appreciation of cultural diversity and of culture's contribution to sustainable development

(Target 4.7)

How Your Support Makes a Difference

Awareness and advocacy are powerful tools for change. When you decide to stay informed by us, you help to amplify the voices of those in need of change, so that they can receive the quality education they deserve. Collective action, no matter how small, can still create an impact. Every time you contribute your attention, time and resources to our cause, we all move a step closer to a future where every child has the opportunity to learn, grow and thrive.
